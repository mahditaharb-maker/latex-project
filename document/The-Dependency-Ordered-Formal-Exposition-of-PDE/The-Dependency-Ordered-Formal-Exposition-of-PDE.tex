\begin{abstract}
\bfseries A step-indexed, dependency-ordered presentation of Appendices A--E
of Evans' \emph{Partial Differential Equations}. The entries are arranged in
ten levels of a topological ordering: every result appears only after all
results it depends on, with circular dependencies broken at the point where
the simpler formulation is introduced first.
\end{abstract}
